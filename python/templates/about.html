About Echo Radio

Your favourite stations, on your own clock.

Why I built this

Echo Radio is a passion project built to let people listen to their favourite radio stations in their local timezone. Built by an Englishman living in San Francisco who really missed waking up to his favourite UK radio shows.

How it works

  • Each station is recorded around the clock in ten-second pieces.
  • When you listen, Echo Radio works out what the station was broadcasting at this wall-clock time in its timezone and plays that. Half-hour zones and daylight-saving changes are handled automatically.
  • The web player lets you rewind through the recent buffer, so a breakfast show can be started from the top even if you tuned in late.
  • Every stream also has a plain MP3 address for smart speakers, car radios and apps such as VLC.

Why “Echo”?

Because sometimes the delay from an echo is exactly what you need.

Under the hood

Liquidsoap records and relays the audio, Icecast serves the MP3 streams, nginx fronts everything on one port, and a small Flask app generates the time-shifted HLS playlists and this interface. Version {{ app_version }}.
